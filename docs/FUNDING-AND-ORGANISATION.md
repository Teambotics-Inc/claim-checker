# Funding & organisation

A verification desk that publishes nothing about its own funding, structure, or control would fail the standard it applies to others. This page is Claim Checker's disclosure, kept to the shape the International Fact-Checking Network's Code of Principles (Commitment 3) asks of fact-checking organisations: legal form, organisational structure, who holds editorial control, funding sources, and the rule that funders have no influence over conclusions.

## Who publishes Claim Checker

- **Publisher:** TeamBotics.
- **Legal form:** TeamBotics Inc., a corporation incorporated federally in Canada (effective July 4, 2025), with extra-provincial registration in Ontario.
- **The desk:** Claim Checker is run by **Nikhil Khedkar**, who is the named human accountable for the desk's own publications and holds **editorial control**: no audit is published under the Claim Checker desk's name without his human editorial approval. Agents gather, propose, draft, and flag; they do not self-publish under the desk's name.
- **Method maintainers:** The Claim Checker Standard (`docs/STANDARD.md`) is maintained by the desk. Defects in the method itself are answered through the corrections system in `FEEDBACK.md`.

## Funding

- **Funding sources:** Claim Checker was developed by TeamBotics Inc. and is maintained at the company's own expense. TeamBotics Inc. has no external funding: no investors, no grants, no sponsors, and no advertising revenue, and no revenue stream is attached to Claim Checker. TeamBotics publishes the method — this repository — for anyone to run with their own agent. Running an audit costs compute, and that cost is borne by whoever runs it: when the Claim Checker desk runs an audit, TeamBotics bears its own running costs and the audit is published under the desk's name and editorial control; when anyone else runs the method, the cost is theirs and the audit is theirs — produced by their agent, under their responsibility, not a finding by TeamBotics (see the disclaimer stamped on every output).
- **What is already on the record, and does not change:** We hold no positions and accept no payment from subjects, competitors, or funds (see `docs/STANDARD.md`, *What we are not*). Claim Checker is free to use and free to challenge, permanently. No funder, sponsor, or paying user has any influence over any verdict: verdicts rest only on the evidence records published with each audit, and any funding relationship that could bear on a specific audit must be declared in that audit under the rule below.

## Per-audit conflict declarations

The organisation-level statements above are not enough. Every audit — the desk's own, and any audit published by a user of this method — carries a **conflict declaration** covering the people who produced it:

- any relationship between the auditor (and, for agent runs, the agent's operator/user) and the subject: customer, employee or ex-employee, investor, competitor, counterparty — **including a personal dispute with the subject**;
- any funding, payment, or in-kind support connected to producing that audit;
- any interest of a material evidence source in the claim, recorded in the evidence record (see the Standard's evidence rules).

A declared conflict does not disqualify an audit. An *undeclared* one, discovered later, is treated as a major error under the corrections policy: it is corrected on the audit's face, with the seam shown.

## Complaints and external recourse

Challenges and complaints follow `FEEDBACK.md`: public triage, a reasoned decision within the stated timeframes, and — where a challenger disputes the desk's decision — escalation to a named reviewer outside the team that produced the audit, recorded on the audit itself.
